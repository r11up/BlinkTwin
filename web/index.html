Getting started

  1. Tell it how you slept. Enter when you woke and how long you slept (below, under What I'm doing). Until then the twin assumes you woke at 07:00 after 7.5 hours.
  2. Check in. Rate your sleepiness a few times a day. Check-ins set your personal level; nothing else does that as well.
  3. Optional: start the camera while you work, and take the 3-minute reaction test now and then.

What I'm doing

Your personal model

Check-ins anchor the twin: they set your level, and your blinks and reaction times then track changes from it. Each signal gets its own anchor, so a camera or reaction time that reads you wrong cannot drag the estimate away.

Your data

Research prototype, not a medical device. It does not diagnose anything and must not be used to decide whether you are fit to drive or operate machinery.
